# MovieRatings validation questions

1. Are movies with more observed ratings rated higher than movies with fewer observed ratings?
2. Are newer movies rated differently from older movies?
3. Do male and female viewers rate `Shrek (2001)` differently?
4. What proportion of the 400 movies are rated differently by male and female viewers?
5. Do only children rate `The Lion King (1994)` higher than viewers with siblings?
6. What proportion of the 400 movies are rated differently by only children and viewers with siblings?
7. Do viewers who prefer watching movies socially rate `The Wolf of Wall Street (2013)` higher than viewers who prefer watching alone?
8. What proportion of the 400 movies are rated higher by social viewers than by viewers who prefer watching alone?
9. Does the rating distribution of `Home Alone (1990)` differ from that of `Finding Nemo (2003)`?
10. How many of the eight named franchises exhibit inconsistent ratings among their constituent movies: Star Wars, Harry Potter, The Matrix, Indiana Jones, Jurassic Park, Pirates of the Caribbean, Toy Story, and Batman?
11. Which movies are rated differently by participants above versus below the median sensation-seeking score?
